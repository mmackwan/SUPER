How the estimate works

Figures are in AUD. The forecast compounds the balance annually and adds contributions at year end after applying 15% contributions tax, then deducts admin fees and insurance. Employer super uses the entered rate on eligible pay before sacrifice. A fixed sacrifice stays the same each year. A percentage sacrifice grows with pay. Today’s dollars discount the final balance for inflation.

This is a scenario estimate. It excludes contribution caps and carry-forward amounts, the maximum employer contribution base, extra taxes such as Division 293, government payments and changes in future rules. The retirement age sets the projection horizon. Russell presets use the published iQ Super – Employer net account deductions, applied annually to the projected balance before fees. The model holds fee rates constant and excludes fee refunds, negotiated rates and changes to premiums. Investment fees and the Trustee Administration Fee belong in the net return assumption.
